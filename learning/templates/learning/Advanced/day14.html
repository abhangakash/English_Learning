📖 3. Reading : Diagnostic Assessment

Self-assessment is not the same as self-criticism. Effective self-assessment is diagnostic — it locates where effort should be directed, not where failure occurred. Research in applied linguistics confirms that learners who self-assess regularly with specific criteria — vocabulary range, grammatical accuracy, discourse coherence — progress significantly faster than those who assess vaguely.

The most useful self-assessment tool is a recording. You cannot objectively evaluate your spoken English from memory alone. The recording does not lie, and it does not flatter. It shows you exactly where you stand — and exactly where to go.

1️⃣ What is the difference between self-assessment and self-criticism?

Meaning: Persistent and determined in holding on to a goal

Usage: "Tenacious learners do not stop at difficulty — they recalibrate and continue."
