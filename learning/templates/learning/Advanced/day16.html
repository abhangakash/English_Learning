MANAGING DIFFICULT QUESTIONS

💬 "The question is not the problem. Your response to it is your opportunity."
💡 TIP OF THE DAY

When facing a difficult question, use the PREP technique: Point (state your position), Reason (give the primary reason), Example (provide evidence), Point (restate your position). This gives you an automatic 30-45 second framework that sounds composed and structured even when you are thinking on your feet.

📖 3. Reading : Managing Pressure

The most dangerous moment in a professional interview or viva is not the hardest question — it is the moment of panic before the answer. Research in performance psychology shows that high performers and underperformers often have equivalent knowledge: what separates them is the ability to manage physiological stress and access that knowledge under pressure. Breath control, deliberate pacing, and structured response frameworks (like PREP or STAR) are not tricks — they are professional tools. The advanced communicator treats difficult questions not as threats to their credibility, but as invitations to demonstrate the depth of their thinking.

🧠 Grammar Hack: Avoid 'I Don't Have the Answer'

This phrase sounds unprepared and defeats credibility in professional settings. Replace with a composed, time-bound commitment.

❌ I don't have the answer.
✅ That is an excellent point — I will confirm the exact data and get back to you by 3 PM today.

❌ I'm not sure about that.
✅ I want to give you an accurate answer. May I revert to you with the precise figures after checking?


Rule: Never leave a professional question with a dead end — always offer a specific follow-up action.
