📖 3. Reading Activity : Precision in Technical Writing

Reading Passage:

In technical writing, precision is not a stylistic preference — it is a professional obligation. Vague language creates ambiguity, and ambiguity in engineering documentation has led to real-world failures. When a specification says 'the component should be tested periodically', the reader has no guidance. When it says 'the component shall be load-tested every 500 operating hours', there is no room for misinterpretation. Advanced writers understand that the most powerful revision tool is not grammar-checking software — it is the question: 'Could this sentence mean something other than what I intend?'

Write a 130-Word Technical Abstract

🧠 6. Grammar Hack: Avoid 'Do the Needful'

📖 Why to avoid "Do the Needful":

This phrase is unique to Indian English and unintelligible globally. Always specify the action clearly.

❌ Incorrect: "Please do the needful."

✅ Correct: "Please update the spreadsheet and send it to me by 5 PM."


❌ Incorrect: "Kindly do the needful and revert."

✅ Correct: "Please confirm your availability and email me by tomorrow."


Rule: Every instruction should answer 'do WHAT, by WHEN, to WHOM.'
