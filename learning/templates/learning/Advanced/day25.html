TIP OF THE DAY

The most common interview mistake is answering the question asked rather than the question behind the question. When a panel asks 'What is the biggest challenge in your project?', the question behind the question is 'Can you identify problems, think critically, and respond constructively?' Answer the second question.

🎧 1. Listening Activity : STAR Responses

🎯Focus
STAR Responses — how to structure interview answers with Situation, Task, Action, Result.
📝Listening Text
"During my final year project, the motor driver we had designed was producing 15% more heat than the simulation predicted. My task was to identify the root cause and resolve it before our evaluation. I systematically tested each sub-circuit and found a higher-than-expected resistance in the transistor base drive circuit. I redesigned the drive stage with lower-impedance components. The thermal output dropped to within 3% of the simulation model, and the project received the highest evaluation grade in our batch."

📖 3. Reading Activity : Viva as Dialogue

Reading Passage:

A viva examination is not an interrogation — it is a structured dialogue. The examiner is not trying to expose your ignorance; they are trying to map the boundaries of your understanding. Understanding this reframes every difficult question from a threat into an opportunity. When you cannot answer a question fully, the most impressive response is not silence, guessing, or making up an answer — it is a structured admission of the boundary: 'I understand the principle clearly, but I would need to review the specific values before giving you an accurate answer.' This response demonstrates intellectual honesty, which examiners value far more than fabricated confidence.

✍️ 4. Writing Activity : STAR Interview Response

Write a 100-120 Word STAR Response

Write a STAR-format response to: 'Tell me about a technical challenge you solved in your diploma project.' Ensure the Result includes a specific, measurable outcome.

💡 Writing Tip: The Result in a STAR answer is where most candidates lose marks — they describe the action well but fail to state the outcome. Even if the outcome was a learning rather than a measurable achievement, quantify it: 'As a result, the team implemented the corrected protocol, which I subsequently used in all remaining test cycles.'

🧠 6. Grammar Hack: Avoid Overconfidence in Interviews

📖 Why avoid overconfidence : Overconfidence in professional settings raises doubts about your self-awareness. Intellectual humility + demonstrated competence = professional credibility.


❌ Incorrect: "I know everything about this topic."

✅ Correct: "I have a strong foundation in this area and I continue to develop my understanding."


❌ Incorrect: "There is nothing more to learn here."

✅ Correct: "I am confident in the fundamentals and I actively look for advanced applications to deepen my knowledge."


Rule: Intellectual humility + demonstrated competence = professional credibility.
