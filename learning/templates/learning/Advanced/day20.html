💬 "Language is not just how you communicate — it is how you think."
💡 TIP OF THE DAY

Critical thinking is not about being negative — it is about being precise. When you analyse any argument, use the four-question filter: (1) What is the claim? (2) What is the evidence? (3) Is the evidence sufficient and credible? (4) What is missing?
Apply these four questions to every professional proposal, report, or presentation you encounter this week.

🎧 1. Listening Activity : Argument Evaluation

🎯Focus
Argument Evaluation — detecting strong and weak reasoning in spoken arguments.
📝The Argument

📖 3. Reading : Linguistic Awareness

Critical thinking and language are inseparable. The words we use to describe a problem shape the solutions we consider. A factory described as 'having recurring maintenance issues' invites reactive solutions. The same factory described as 'operating without a preventive maintenance framework' invites systemic redesign. Advanced communicators choose the language that opens the most productive solution space.

1️⃣ Explain the difference between how 'recurring maintenance issues' and 'lacking a preventive maintenance framework' shape the response to a problem.

2️⃣ Give one example from your own engineering field where the language used to describe a problem determines the type of solution considered.

3️⃣ Rewrite this limiting statement into a solution-oriented frame: 'Our project is always delayed.'

🔥POWER WORD: INCISIVE (in-SY-siv)

Meaning: Intelligently analytical and clear-thinking

Usage: "Her incisive analysis of the failure report saved the production timeline."

💬IDIOM: "The bottom line"

Meaning: The most important point or conclusion

Example: "The bottom line is that without additional funding, the project cannot be completed on time."

🧠 Grammar Hack: Avoid 'Obviously' and 'Clearly' in Arguments

These words assume the reader agrees — which can be offensive if they do not. They are also lazy: instead of proving something is obvious, prove that it is true.

❌ Obviously, solar energy is the best solution.
✅ Data from comparable installations indicates that solar energy delivers the highest ROI in this climate.

❌ Clearly, the project needs more time.
✅ The current timeline does not allow for the three regulatory approval stages, each requiring a minimum of two weeks.

Rule: Replace 'obviously/clearly' with the evidence that makes it obvious. Prove it, don't just claim it.
