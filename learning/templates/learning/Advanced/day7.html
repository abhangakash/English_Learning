📖 3. Reading Activity : The Power of Narrative

Reading Passage:

Engineers are trained to think in systems, diagrams, and data. But the most impactful engineers are those who can translate that technical thinking into a story that a non-engineer can act on. A story does what a specification cannot: it creates empathy, suspense, and investment. When you tell a decision-maker about a system failure as a narrative — with characters, tension, and resolution — they remember it. When you give them a data table, they file it away. The engineer who masters storytelling gains an influence that pure technical skill cannot.

✍️ 4. Writing Activity : Technical Problem Story

Write a 150-Word Technical Story

Write about solving a technical problem. Include:

  • Situation — what was the context?
  • Complication — what went wrong?
  • Action — what did you do?
  • Resolution — what was the outcome?

🧠 6. Grammar Hack: Avoid Sentence Fragments

📖 Why to avoid sentence fragments:

A sentence must have a subject and a verb. Fragments are incomplete thoughts that mislead readers.

❌ Incorrect (Fragment): "Because the voltage dropped suddenly."

✅ Correct: "The system shut down because the voltage dropped suddenly."


❌ Incorrect (Fragment): "The engineer. Working on the project alone."

✅ Correct: "The engineer was working on the project alone."


Rule: Every sentence must have a subject and a verb. Before submitting any writing, check that every sentence can stand alone with a complete thought.
